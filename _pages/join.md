## PhD and postdoc positions

Positions in the group are funded through our **Villum Young Investigator** project
on designing material functionalities with spatially structured light, and through the
**[SPARKLE](https://www.sparkle-dn.eu/)** Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral network, which
trains PhD students across Europe on polaritonic materials.

Open calls are announced on the [DTU job portal](https://www.dtu.dk/english/about/job-and-career/vacant-positions)
and on our [news page]({{ '/news/' | relative_url }}). Unsolicited applications are welcome too.

## Master and bachelor projects

DTU students interested in a thesis project in theoretical condensed matter physics,
light–matter interaction or first-principles simulations are welcome to get in touch.
