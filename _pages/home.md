We are a condensed matter physics group specialised in theoretical methods for
**ab-initio calculations**. Based at the Technical University of Denmark and
partnered with the Max Planck Institute for the Structure and Dynamics of Matter
in Hamburg, we combine quantum electrodynamics, many-body perturbation theory and
density functional theory to design materials whose properties can be tailored
by light.
