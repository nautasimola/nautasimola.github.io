  2. arXiv Enriching molecular Raman spectroscopy with vibrational strong coupling

  4. arXiv A Hidden Quantum Paraelectric Phase in SrTiO3 Induced by Terahertz Field
    W. Li, H. Kim, X. Wang, J. Luo, S. Latini, D. Shin, et al.
    arXiv preprint (2024)

    Increasing the terahertz field first drives SrTiO3 from quantum paraelectric to ferroelectric, and then back into a hidden quantum paraelectric phase. First-principles dynamics trace this re-entrant behaviour to antiferrodistortive phonon modes.
