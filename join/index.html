We are always happy to hear from curious, motivated people who want to work on light–matter physics.

PhD and postdoc positions

Positions in the group are funded through our Villum Young Investigator project on designing material functionalities with spatially structured light, and through the SPARKLE Marie Skłodowska-Curie doctoral network, which trains PhD students across Europe on polaritonic materials.

Open calls are announced on the DTU job portal and on our news page. Unsolicited applications are welcome too.

Master and bachelor projects

DTU students interested in a thesis project in theoretical condensed matter physics, light–matter interaction or first-principles simulations are welcome to get in touch.
