About the group

Light as a tool to tailor materials

We are a condensed matter physics group specialised in theoretical methods for ab-initio calculations. Based at the Technical University of Denmark and partnered with the Max Planck Institute for the Structure and Dynamics of Matter in Hamburg, we combine quantum electrodynamics, many-body perturbation theory and density functional theory to design materials whose properties can be tailored by light.

  • Cavity materials engineering
  • Strong light–matter interaction
  • Non-equilibrium electron dynamics
  • Optical properties of two-dimensional materials
  • First-principles methods for many-body theory

Selected papers

Full list
  1. Mater. Quantum Technol. Quantum materials engineering by structured cavity vacuum fluctuations
    H. Hübener, E. Viñas Boström, M. Claassen, S. Latini, A. Rubio
    Materials for Quantum Technology 4, 023002 (2024)

    A perspective on how structured cavity vacuum fluctuations can switch the ground state of materials. Three theoretical scenarios illustrate quantum phase transitions induced in the dark, without any photons in the cavity.

    Article
  2. PNAS The ferroelectric photo ground state of SrTiO3: Cavity materials engineering
    S. Latini, D. Shin, S. A. Sato, C. Schäfer, U. De Giovannini, H. Hübener, et al.
    Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences 118, e2105618118 (2021)

    In the strong light-matter coupling regime, cavity vacuum fluctuations stabilize ferroelectricity as the ground state of SrTiO3. We call this light-matter hybrid ground state a material photo ground state.

    Article
  3. PRL Phonoritons as Hybridized Exciton-Photon-Phonon Excitations in a Monolayer h-BN Optical Cavity
    S. Latini, U. De Giovannini, E. J. Sie, N. Gedik, H. Hübener, A. Rubio
    Physical Review Letters 126, 227401 (2021)

    Monolayer hBN in an optical cavity hosts phonoritons, hybrid excitations of excitons, photons and phonons. We predict clear spectroscopic signatures and propose a realistic route to observe them.

    Article
  4. Commun. Chem. Real-time observation of a correlation-driven sub 3 fs charge migration in ionised adenine
    Erik P. Månsson, Simone Latini, Fabio Covito, Vincent Wanie, Mara Galli, Enrico Perfetto, et al.
    Communications Chemistry 4, 73 (2021)

    Attosecond pump-probe spectroscopy captures charge migration in ionized adenine within 3 femtoseconds. Many-body simulations trace this ultrafast motion to electron correlation.

    Article
  5. Science
    Science Programmable hyperbolic polaritons in van der Waals semiconductors
    A. J. Sternbach, S. H. Chae, S. Latini, A. A. Rikhter, Y. Shao, B. Li, et al.
    Science 371, 617–620 (2021)

    Ultrafast optical pumping turns on hyperbolic dispersion in the van der Waals semiconductor WSe2, creating a transient waveguide for polaritons. This gives an all-optical switch for subwavelength light confinement.

    Article
